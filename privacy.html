Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

1. Overview

MoneyDecoded is a software tool for creating, reviewing, and managing educational finance videos. This policy describes the intended data processing for its local installation and optional TikTok integration. It must be updated if the deployed product changes.

2. Data processed

Depending on the features enabled, MoneyDecoded may process video files, captions, titles, descriptions, publishing decisions, publication identifiers, error logs, and identifiers needed to connect a TikTok account. OAuth access and refresh tokens may be processed to maintain authorized access. The software does not require users to provide their TikTok password.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Data is processed to provide video generation, account connection, publishing workflows, duplicate-publication protection, troubleshooting, and security. Where applicable, processing is based on providing requested functionality, legitimate security interests, or consent/authorization for optional integrations.

4. TikTok integration

If connected, TikTok authorization is used only within the permissions the user grants, such as uploading or publishing videos and checking publication status. Available permissions and actual publication behavior depend on TikTok's approved access and platform policies. Users may revoke access through TikTok account settings.

5. Third-party processors

Where enabled, prompts or scripts may be sent to Anthropic for script generation, and narration text may be sent to ElevenLabs for text-to-speech. Video publishing sends selected video content and associated metadata to TikTok. Each service processes data under its own terms and privacy policies.

6. Storage and security

Local installations may store generated media, workflow metadata, and publishing history on the operator's computer. API keys and OAuth tokens should be stored securely outside public repositories or frontend code. Reasonable access controls and technical safeguards should be implemented before enabling third-party account access.

7. Retention and deletion

Local generated files and publishing records are retained until removed by the operator or an applicable retention process. TikTok authorization can be revoked using TikTok's controls; related local credentials should then be deleted. For data-access or deletion requests, use the contact channel below.

8. Sharing

Data is shared with connected services only as needed to perform enabled functions. This policy does not authorize sale of personal information.

9. International transfers

External services may process data outside the user's country. Any required transfer safeguards and disclosures must be evaluated for the actual deployment.

10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection. Requests will be assessed under applicable law.

11. Contact

To contact the operator about privacy, open an issue at MoneyDecoded Legal GitHub Issues, without sharing private credentials or sensitive personal information publicly. A dedicated private contact channel should be added before external user onboarding.

12. Changes

This policy may be revised to reflect actual integrations, retention practices, and legal requirements. The effective date will be updated.
